# VIO Data Sources and Synthetic Data Boundary

## Approved Direction

Use accurate, publicly available university facts in VIO where the information is relevant and its source can be recorded. Public availability is not a reason to replace real institutional context with a fictional university or catalogue. Do not infer missing facts or present stale material as current.

Use clearly synthetic data for private or unavailable workflow records, especially student reports, confirmations, model decisions, lecturer replies, authority responses, and report delivery. These records must not describe a real person or a real alleged incident, and any delivery test must use isolated test recipients. Never send test reports to real lecturers or authorities.

For each imported public dataset, record its source URL, publisher, access date, source publication/update date when available, fields copied, and limitations. Prefer factual fields and concise attribution; do not copy long copyrighted descriptions when a title/code/link is enough. Never scrape behind a login.

## Seed and Test Data Rules

- `database/seed.sql` may contain sourced public institutional facts and clearly marked, undecided configuration placeholders.
- Do not put invented faculties, departments, course codes, course registrations, timetable slots, student email domains, lecturers, authorities, or routing contacts into the public-data seed.
- Do not place real allegations or real student records in seed or test data.
- Add synthetic workflow fixtures separately, clearly label them as synthetic, and ensure the environment cannot send email or delivery links to real recipients.
- A field without a verified source remains absent/NULL or is represented as unavailable; it is not guessed to make the application look complete.
