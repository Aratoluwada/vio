## How To Use This

- Treat the official admission portal as current evidence that a programme is advertised for 2026/2027, subject to the portal's wording and dates.
- Treat third-party college assignments as candidate mappings. Verify each college and department against a current FUNAAB college/department page or official handbook before using the mapping for course eligibility or routing.
- A programme is not a course unit. Do not invent `courses.code`, level, semester, curriculum, or timetable slots from programme names.
- Do not infer college ownership from the broad official admission subject group.
- Record later corrections with source URL, access date, page/document date, exact field corrected, and reason.

## Remaining Data Needed

1. A current official college list and college-to-department directory.
2. Official course forms/handbooks containing course-unit codes, level, semester, and programme/department links.
3. Verified course-level ownership, especially for courses shared across programmes/colleges.
4. Baseline timetable extraction and validation against these official course-unit records.
5. Student course-registration verification source. Admission offerings do not prove an individual student's registration.
