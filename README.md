# vio – Voice It Out
Every Student Deserves to Be Heard.
vio is a student-centered platform designed to provide a structured and accessible way for university students to report academic challenges and share feedback.
From missed lectures and incomplete course coverage to concerns about assessment practices, vio helps students communicate their experiences through an organized feedback system.
The platform aims to promote transparency, accountability and constructive communication between students and relevant university authorities.
Reports will go through appropriate review and moderation processes to encourage responsible feedback and protect students' privacy.
